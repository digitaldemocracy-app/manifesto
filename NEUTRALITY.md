# Declaration of Neutrality and Interests

I wrote the manifesto and signed it with my name. Anyone who asks for the trust of every side should say, before being asked, which side they are on and where the money comes from. Here it is.

## Which side I am on

No party's. Digital Democracy is not left or right: it is an instrument, and it serves whoever uses it. I endorse no candidates and no parties — not here, not on my personal accounts. I criticize mechanisms, not people. My vote is secret, like everyone's. That is exactly what the platform stands for.

## What can be checked

- I am not a member of any political party. I never have been.
- I have never donated to any election campaign.
- I am not a candidate for anything.
- I hold no public office. I am not a civil servant, a member of the military, a judge or a prosecutor.

In Brazil, party membership and campaign donations are public records of the Superior Electoral Court (TSE). Anyone can look them up. You do not have to take my word for it: check.

## Where the money comes from

For now, from me. Digital Democracy began as a personal initiative, but it is made to be carried by everyone who cares to — and I hope that soon it will stand on its own, without depending on me. Today, I work on it in my free time, unpaid, and I pay for the website and the infrastructure out of my own pocket. There is no other source: no donations, no investors, no sponsors, no money from any party, candidate, campaign or government. Nothing I write or build here belongs to an employer.

To build the platform, we will need money. When it comes, everything will be open and instant: every account, every deposit, where each one came from, how much and when — published the moment it happens, not afterwards.

## What I promise

1. **Every source of money will be public, in real time.** Donation, sponsorship, grant, investment or contract: who gave, how much and when, before the money is used.
2. **No money from parties, candidates or campaigns.** Ever.
3. **No campaigning.** I will not use the manifesto or the platform to ask for votes for anyone — including me.
4. **I will not run for office** while I lead this project. If one day I want to, I will step down first, and say so here.

## If anything changes

This page is valid as long as it is true. If I join a party, donate, accept money, take office or change my mind about any line above, I will update this page before doing it — not after. Every change stays in the history of this repository. Nothing is erased in silence.

— Murilo Elias
Florianópolis, November 2026

_Reviewed at least every six months, even if nothing changes._
