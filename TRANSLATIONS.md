# Translations

The official text of the manifesto is the English version in [`README.md`](README.md). Every translation is made from the English, using the Portuguese original ([`pt/README.md`](pt/README.md)) as the reference of meaning when a sentence is ambiguous.

Each language has its own folder, named with its ISO 639-1 code, holding `README.md` (the manifesto) and, when translated, `PRINCIPLES.md`, `FAQ.md` and `NEUTRALITY.md`. `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `SIGNATURES.md` and `LICENSE` exist only in English, at the root.

Status values: **Pending** (no text yet) · **Draft** (translated, not yet reviewed by a native speaker) · **Reviewed** (reviewed by at least one native speaker) · **Done**.

Why these ten languages: they are the ten most spoken languages in the world by total speakers (Ethnologue 2025). Together they reach roughly two thirds of humanity. Urdu is first on the waiting list. Translations into any other language are welcome — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

Right-to-left languages (Arabic, and in the future Urdu, Persian, Hebrew) wrap the whole text in `<div dir="rtl">` so that GitHub renders paragraphs aligned to the right. See [`ar/README.md`](ar/README.md) for the template.
